# Business Memo: Early Warning Signs of Churn
**To:** Product & Marketing Teams  
**From:** Data Science Team  
**Date:** Oct 24, 2023  
**Subject:** We found 3 main reasons why customers are leaving.

## Overview
We analyzed our 2,400 customers to find out why they stop buying. Our average churn rate is roughly **47%**. However, certain groups are leaving much faster than others.

## Top 3 Risk Areas (Hypotheses)

### 1. The "Google Search" Problem
**Observation:** Customers who found us through **Google Search** churn at a rate of **50.43%**.
**Why?** Users coming from ads might just be looking for a one-time deal, unlike "Organic" users (who only churn at 39%).
**Recommendation:** We should check if our Google Ads are promising something we aren't delivering.

### 2. The "Baby Care" Drop-off
**Observation:** The **Baby Care** category has the highest churn of all products at **50.72%**.
**Why?** Babies grow up fast! Once the baby outgrows the product, the parent leaves.
**Recommendation:** We need to email these parents with "Toddler" products before they leave.

### 3. The "Ghost" Users
**Observation:** Customers who leave usually haven't visited our site in **26 days**. Customers who stay visited us **9 days** ago.
**Recommendation:** If a customer hasn't visited in 14 days, send them a "We Miss You" email immediately.

## Next Steps
We will now move to **Part 2**, where we will group these customers into "Loyal", "At Risk", and "Lost" segments so we can target them with specific offers.
